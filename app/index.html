How this differs from a vote or an oracle

Validators read a scoreboard page as untrusted material and agree on one coarse result bucket and two goal counts. They never pick a winner, rank a table, or move funds. Points, head-to-head, goal difference, the recorded-first rule and the medals are computed by the contract from stored results.
